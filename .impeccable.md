## Design Context

### Users
Dejavue is for admins and moderators running Discord help and support forums, especially developer tools, SaaS communities, game studios, and open-source projects. They use it to catch repeated questions as they are posted, reuse already-solved answers, run a clean solved workflow, and turn Discord support history into a searchable public knowledge base.

### Brand Personality
Smart, calm, and a little magical. The product should feel like it quietly makes a community smarter: capable and polished without being loud, gamer-like, or gimmicky. The voice is concise, confident, helpful, and lightly playful around the idea of déjà vu.

### Aesthetic Direction
A refined, modern productivity-tool aesthetic closer to Linear, Notion, and Vercel than to a typical Discord bot. Use the locked Ink, indigo, violet, light-violet, mint, Cloud, and Fog palette from `docs/brand/DESIGN_BRIEF.md`, Space Grotesk for display, Inter for body copy, and JetBrains Mono only for small technical accents. The signature visual metaphor is “The Echo”: a crisp solved item paired with a faint duplicate behind it. Favor generous negative space, rounded Discord-adjacent geometry, restrained gradients, and precise dark surfaces. Avoid loud gamer styling, clip-art robots, literal eyes, excessive glow, glassmorphism, and shadow-heavy card stacks.

### Design Principles
1. Make the duplicate-to-answer loop immediately understandable through state, hierarchy, and purposeful motion.
2. Keep the product quietly confident: one memorable hero moment beats scattered effects.
3. Use the Echo metaphor consistently, with duplicate elements always softer and lower-opacity than their resolved source.
4. Match Discord closely enough to feel native while preserving Dejavue’s violet-and-mint identity.
5. Protect clarity, performance, and accessibility at every breakpoint, including a complete reduced-motion experience.
